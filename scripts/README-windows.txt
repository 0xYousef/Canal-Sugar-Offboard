CANAL SUGAR OFFBOARDING
=======================

Prefer pictures? Open the file "Setup guide (with pictures)" in this folder.

The first time (about 2 minutes)
--------------------------------
1. Right-click the zip file you downloaded and choose "Extract All".
   Then press "Extract".
2. In the new folder, right-click the file called
        Install Canal Sugar Offboarding
   and choose "Run as administrator".
3. Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?"
   Press "Yes".
4. A black window shows 3 steps and then "All done!".
   Your browser opens the portal by itself.

You can delete the folder you downloaded.

Signing in the first time
-------------------------
Sign in with the email and the starter password you were sent. The portal then
asks you to choose your own password: type it twice and press "Save password".
Write it down somewhere safe. After that, always sign in with your own password.
There is no sign-up page: if you have no account yet, ask the Owner to add you.


Every day after that
--------------------
Double-click the "Canal Sugar Offboarding" icon on your desktop.
(No administrator needed any more.)
When you are finished, just close the browser. That is all.

(You need no internet and no administrator password.
 It works on Windows 10 and 11.)


If something is not working
---------------------------
* "Windows protected your PC":
  press "More info", then "Run anyway". Windows only asks this once.
* There is no "Run as administrator" in the menu: right-click the file and
  look for "Show more options" first (Windows 11).
* Nothing opens: open the folder named "More" (inside the program folder),
  and double-click "Open with a window (for problems)". Read the message in
  the black window, or send a photo of it to your IT team.
* The page looks empty or broken: press the keys Ctrl and F5 together.
* Your antivirus blocks it: ask your IT team to allow the folder
  C:\Users\<your name>\program\Canal-Sugar-Offboarding


About your information
----------------------
Everything you enter is saved on THIS computer, in your web browser.
  * Always open the portal from the desktop icon, so it opens in the same browser.
  * Another computer will not see your information. Each computer has its own.
  * Please export your sheet from Reports at the end of every day and keep the file somewhere safe.
  * Clearing your browser's "site data" or "history and cookies" erases the information.


To stop the program straight away
---------------------------------
Open the "More" folder and double-click "Stop Canal Sugar Offboarding".
(It also stops by itself a few minutes after you close the browser.)
