Something not working?

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Press More info, then Run anyway. Windows only asks once, because the program is not signed.

Nothing opens

Open the More folder inside the program folder and double-click Open with a window (for problems). Read the message in the black window, or send a photo of it to your IT team.

The page looks empty or broken

Hold the Ctrl key and press F5.

My information disappeared

Your information is saved in your web browser on this computer. Always open the portal from the desktop icon so it uses the same browser. Clearing "history and cookies" erases it, so export your sheet from Reports at the end of every day.

My antivirus blocks it

Ask your IT team to allow the folder C:\Users\<your name>\program\Canal-Sugar-Offboarding.
